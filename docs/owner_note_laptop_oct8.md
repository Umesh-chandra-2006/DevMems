# Note for the Owner: the laptop until about 07:00 on Oct 9

The two experiment runs are running on this laptop right now. Keep these rules; a wrong move loses hours, not the whole experiment.

1. Keep it plugged in and awake. Never let it sleep, hibernate or shut down. Sleep is already set to never; do not change the power plan.
2. Closing the lid on AC is safe (the lid action is set to Do nothing). Do not close it on battery.
3. Travelling: put it in a bag only with the lid closed and the power cable connected; a laptop that sleeps or overheats in a bag stops the runs.
4. Switching Wi-Fi is safe; the runs wait for the network and carry on. Do NOT use the phone hotspot: it was measured slower. If you can, stay on the same network.
5. Closing or restarting the Claude app may kill background programs (this happened once at about 00:10). A watchdog now restarts them within 5 minutes, but each restart costs some lost minutes.
6. Do not install updates and do not restart Windows. Windows Update is paused; if it asks to restart, choose later.
7. Do not open, move or delete anything under D:\DevMems, and do not start another copy of the runs.
8. Closing browser tabs of the viewer is fine; the viewer is read only.
9. If the laptop shuts down or restarts: plug it in, log in, wait 5 minutes (the watchdog starts at logon and every 5 minutes), then tell the PM and me the time it happened. Do not start anything by hand.
10. If anything looks stuck for more than 15 minutes, send me the time; do not press keys in any black window.
